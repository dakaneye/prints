# Spiral fidget (hexagonal)

Two-part interlocking helical fidget — a hexagonal outer shell with a
matching insert that spirals along its axis. Twist motion (not a spring
stretch). Pure download from MakerWorld — no custom CAD on this one.

See `downloaded/SOURCES.md` for the URL, author, and license.

## Print recipe

| Part | Dims | Print time | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Outer hexagonal shell | 46 × 40 × 66 mm | ~2 h | Print upright; minimal supports needed for hex faces |
| Insert | 37 × 37 × 72 mm | ~2 h | Print upright on the flat end |

Filament: SUNLU PLA+ 2.0, any color. Standard 0.2 mm profile. The
helical fit is tight by design — the parts may need a few twists to
free up after assembly.
