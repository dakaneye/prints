# Infinite cube

Sculptural display cube with internal voids creating an "infinite" visual
effect. Static piece (no moving parts), printed as a single solid with
supports. Pure download from MakerWorld — no custom CAD on this one.

See `downloaded/SOURCES.md` for the URL, author, and license.

## Print recipe

| Filament | Print time | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| SUNLU PLA+ 2.0 (any color) | ~5–7 h | ~111 mm cube; rotate 45° on the bed to save support material (~26 g less, ~50 min faster). Standard 0.2 mm profile. |

White or grey filament emphasises the internal voids best; oak wood
hides the depth.
