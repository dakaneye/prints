# Infinite cube

Sculptural display cube with internal voids creating an "infinite" visual
effect. Static piece (no moving parts), printed as a single solid with
supports. Pure download from MakerWorld — no custom CAD on this one.

See `downloaded/SOURCES.md` for the URL, author, and license.

## Print recipe

| Filament | Print time | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| SUNLU PLA+ 2.0 (any color) | ~5–7 h | ~111 mm cube. Print **flat on a face** for stability — corner-down orientations save support material but are tippy and prone to mid-print failure. Standard 0.2 mm profile. |

White or grey filament emphasises the internal voids best; oak wood
hides the depth.

**Lesson from print attempt 2026-04-25:** corner-down (45° rotation)
failed mid-print — the small bed-contact area + tall lever arm let a
support contact go and the print collapsed into spaghetti. Print flat.
