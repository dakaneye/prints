# Print log — pitcher spigot valve

Spring-loaded, lever-operated poppet (PETG body). Supersedes two earlier
attempts: a quarter-turn plug valve and a flexing TPU diaphragm, neither of
which sealed reliably. The current design seals with a spring-pressed soft
stopper on a seat — positive shut-off at any water level.

## Prototype 1 (planned)

Goal: body prints watertight chamber-up; the spring shuts the stopper firmly
(full and near-empty); the lever pulls down to lift it ~4–5 mm and pour; the
glass mount seals with the two gaskets.
