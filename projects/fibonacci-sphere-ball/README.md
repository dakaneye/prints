# Fibonacci sphere ball

Hollow geometric ball with a Fibonacci-lattice pattern of cutouts on the
surface — like an "airless" basketball or tennis ball. Single solid
print, no moving parts. The MakerWorld download includes an OpenSCAD
source plus 5 pre-sized examples.

Intended use: toddler-friendly play ball for a 2.5-year-old.

See `downloaded/SOURCES.md` for the URL, author, and license.

## Variants in the download

| Variant STL | Size | Toddler pick? |
|---|---|---|
| `FibonacciSphereTableTennis.stl` | 40 mm | borderline small |
| `FibonacciSphereGolf.stl` | 42.7 mm | borderline small |
| **`FibonacciSphereTennis.stl`** | **65 mm** | **yes — sweet spot, tennis-ball feel** |
| **`FibonacciStressBall.stl`** | **75 mm** | **yes — biggest/softest grip** |
| `FibonacciSphereDisplay.stl` | 100 mm | display piece; too heavy as a thrown toy |

## Print recipe

| Filament | Print time | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| SUNLU PLA+ 2.0 (white or oak wood) | ~1.5–2 h (65 mm) | Low infill (5–10%) keeps it light and forgiving when she throws it. No supports — the Fibonacci cutouts let the sphere self-vent during printing. |

Light colors are easier for a toddler to spot when the ball rolls under
furniture; avoid black/grey for this one.
