## Recommended starting points

These are go-to designs for the bin types this drawer needs. URLs aren't
linked here on purpose — fetch and paste them into entries above as you
download.

- **Generic bins (any cell size, configurable height):** Gridfinity Rebuilt
  on MakerWorld or via the kennetek OpenSCAD generator
- **Pen / marker holder:** search MakerWorld for "Gridfinity pen holder"
  (vertical 1x2 with cylindrical pockets is the common shape)
- **Cards / business cards:** "Gridfinity card holder" or "Gridfinity
  playing card holder" (often 2x2x4U)
- **Glasses case insert:** likely a custom 4x2x6U bin with a soft cradle —
  may need to print blank and modify, or generate parametrically
- **Guitar pick organizer:** "Gridfinity guitar pick" — community designs
  exist for various pick counts
- **Cable / earbud organizer:** "Gridfinity cable" — many shapes,
  pick one that matches the wrap style
