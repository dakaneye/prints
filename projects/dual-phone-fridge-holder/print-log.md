# Print log — dual phone fridge holder

## 2026-07-03 — first print (grey), print OK, magnets insufficient

- Printed standing, rotated 45° on the plate to fit, 5 mm brim — printed
  fine, no adhesion or wobble issues.
- 20× 6×2 mm assorted-set discs press-fit + CA as designed. On the fridge
  side the holder **slips down under phone load** — magnets grip normal to
  the surface but shear on the paint gives way. Currently duct-taped as a
  stopgap.
- Post-mortem: the spec's holding budget derated ~2×; reality on painted
  vertical steel is more like 5–10× (weak assorted-set grade, paint-layer
  gap hits 2 mm-thick magnets hard, and any bow in the 200 mm plate lifts
  some magnets clear of the surface entirely).
- Next: stack a second 6×2 disc on each magnet (no reprint, ~1.5×), and if
  that still slips, regenerate for 6× ⌀20×3 N52 discs.
