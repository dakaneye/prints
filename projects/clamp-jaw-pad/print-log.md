## Known deviations from the original

Functional and accepted as good enough, but **not a 1:1 replacement**:
it is thicker than the original (extra `BACK_WALL` for strength) and
sticks out farther along the jaw. To make it 1:1, measure the
original's true overall thickness and seat depth, then trim `BACK_WALL`
/ `OUTER_LENGTH`. Deferred — see the spec's "Status" section.
